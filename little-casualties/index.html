// ABOUT THE GAME

Little Casualties is a chaotic, physics-based puzzle game where you guide named units through deadly environments using simple tools with unpredictable outcomes.

You place bridges, catapults, trampolines, and parachute dispensers to try and get enough units safely to the exit. Everything else is collateral.

Each level becomes a story of near success, accidental disaster, and small victories that barely hold together.

Most will not make it. Their names still matter.
